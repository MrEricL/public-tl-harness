Chapter 3: Fire on the Stargazing Terrace

When they returned to the Azure Cloud Sect, the eastern sky had just turned cold and white. The gate had not been swallowed by the flames, but every stone beast had fallen inward, as if bowing to an intruder. The Tidal Mirror shard pointed toward the rear mountain, and each turn briefly revealed a blue stairway in the smoke.

The elders gathered the disciples to hold the front hall, while Gu Ling handed the guild seal to the gate warden. The warden drew his sword at the sight of it. He admitted that he had opened the mountain road for the shadow, but insisted that he had done it to rescue his brother, who was trapped in the ancient city. Shen Yan did not condemn him at once. He only asked how the warden knew about the seal at the Stargazing Terrace.

The answer came from the second moon above the Stargazing Terrace. It hung in the daylight, and beneath it stood an old man in gray robes, writing the disciples' names into the Tidal Mirror. Each name he wrote made the fire at the gate rise higher. Give him the shard, the old man said, and everyone would be safe.

Shen Yan tossed the shard into the air and used its blue light to see four trails of water left in the courtyard. He followed them with the Starstream Step: the first step avoided a moon blade, the second gave Gu Ling an opening to cut the mirror thread, and the third carried him to the edge of the Stargazing Terrace. The old man expected him to keep moving forward, but Shen Yan stopped outside the starlight and left the final step to the mountain beneath his feet.

The mountain's echo passed through the stone platform. A seal rose from underground like a closing hand and seized the Tidal Mirror. Forced into view, the gray-robed old man proved to be the Stargazing Elder, who had vanished many years before. The flames went out as the mirror shattered. The warden lowered his sword, and Gu Ling decided to remain with the Azure Cloud Sect to investigate the guild. After sunrise, Shen Yan wrote the Starstream Step into the gatekeeper's register, took the new map, and walked toward the world beyond the mountain.
