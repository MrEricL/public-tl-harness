Chapter 2: Echoes Beneath Mirror Lake

Before dawn, the sky faded from deep blue to gray as Shen Yan and the girl in the straw cape reached Mirror Lake Town. The water lay so still that there was not a breath of wind, yet every fishing net along the shore was pulled tight in the same direction, as if a beast below had seized the knots. Shen Yan pressed the Azure Cloud Sect token against a stone marker, and a white line surfaced at the center of the lake.

The girl's name was Gu Ling, and she was the town's last lamp keeper. She guided Shen Yan in a small boat to the end of the white line. The second moon was no illusion, she told him, but an ancient city turned upside down beneath the lake. Whenever anyone tried to approach, the city gate shifted with the moonlight; even old people who knew the waterways by heart could not find the way back.

They dove beneath the surface, passed through a layer of warm blue light, and landed before the ancient city's stone gate. Shen Yan unfolded the elder's map and found seven stars carved beside the door. Gu Ling stepped in the wrong place, and water pressed in from every side. Shen Yan quickly used the Starstream Step, making the stones beneath his feet light one after another until the pressure withdrew.

There was no street beyond the gate, only a courtyard suspended in darkness. The shadow stood in its center, holding the black needle stamped with the guild seal. He admitted that he had been ordered to find the Tidal Mirror, but said the guild in Mirror Lake Town had already been replaced. Its true master, he said, was hiding behind the Azure Cloud Sect.

Gu Ling refused to believe him and advanced with her knife drawn. Fine chains suddenly fell through the darkness. Shen Yan used the Starstream Step to circle the courtyard's four stone pillars and cut the mechanism locking their escape. The three of them rushed through a split water gate and surfaced together. A shard of the Tidal Mirror clung to Shen Yan's palm, reflecting a mountain gate in flames.
