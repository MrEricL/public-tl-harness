# Starstream Crossing style guide

- Use clean, contemporary literary English in past tense and close third person.
- Preserve concrete images, cause and effect, and the order of each source scene.
- Keep action brisk; let physical details carry atmosphere instead of adding explanation.
- Use title case for named places, sects, techniques, and objects, following the glossary exactly.
- Keep dialogue plain and restrained. Avoid invented lore, ornamental epithets, and translator notes.
- Use standard English punctuation and paragraph breaks. Do not leave Chinese characters in delivery text.
