# Sanitized comparison examples

These examples paraphrase the larger divergences observed by blind judges.
They intentionally omit source wording, candidate wording, names, glossary
entries, and anything that could reconstruct a chapter.

1. **Formatting divergence.** One candidate repeatedly retained source-style
   full-width brackets in a long marked-dialogue passage; the other normalized
   the passage to the target's square-bracket convention. Judges consistently
   noticed the formatting difference even when they found the underlying prose
   equivalent.

2. **Terminology divergence.** One candidate used a transparent romanization
   for a named location while the other used an established localized
   rendering. Model families judged the choice differently, contributing to
   the baseline 5 / Harness 1 / tie 14 terminology split.

Neither example is a substitute for the withheld corpus or a verbatim
evaluation packet.
